We measure first- and second-moment discrepancies separately within each
coalition-size slice and then aggregate them with equal weight across sizes.
Let $P=I_n-\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\mathsf T}/n$ and
$u_S=\sqrt{n/[s(n-s)]}(\mathbf{1}_S-s\mathbf{1}/n)$ for $|S|=s$.
For the sampled multiset $\mathcal B_s$ of size $T_s$, define
\[
\bar u_s=\frac{1}{T_s}\sum_{S\in\mathcal B_s}u_S,
\qquad
M_s=\frac{1}{T_s}\sum_{S\in\mathcal B_s}u_Su_S^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Our across-size RMS discrepancies are
\[
D_1=\sqrt{\frac{1}{|\mathcal S_{\mathrm{int}}|}
\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{int}}}\|\bar u_s\|_2^2},
\qquad
D_2=\sqrt{\frac{1}{|\mathcal S_{\mathrm{int}}|}
\sum_{s\in\mathcal S_{\mathrm{int}}}
\left\|M_s-\frac{P}{n-1}\right\|_F^2}.
\]
For Airport, $n=100$ and $\mathcal S_{\mathrm{int}}=\{2,\ldots,98\}$;
the boundary coalitions are evaluated separately. Sizes are weighted equally,
rather than in proportion to $T_s$, so the summaries do not additionally
weight discrepancies by the size allocation. The within-size discrepancies
can nevertheless depend on the number of samples allocated to each size.

Figure~4(a) shows that INSIDE-Coalition reduces the first-moment discrepancy
relative to OFA (IID), whereas the complete cyclic orbits of INSIDE-Orbit
achieve exact first-moment balance. Figure~4(b) compares the second-moment
discrepancy. These diagnostics correspond to the moment factors in
Theorem~5.5, rather than the full error bound, which also depends on
size-specific coefficients and a residual term.
Figure~4(c) shows a positive pooled association between $D_2$ and estimation
RMSE (Spearman $\rho=0.973$, 45 method--budget--repeat observations),
providing empirical support for the second-moment component of Theorem~5.5.
This association is descriptive: utility budget affects both quantities.
After subtracting each method--budget group's mean log discrepancy and mean
log RMSE, the residual Spearman correlation is only $0.050$; the pooled
trend therefore does not establish an independent effect of $D_2$ on error.
Together with the First-only ablation in Figure~3(a), the first-moment
measurements provide complementary evidence about the first-moment term.

\paragraph{Figure 4: Moment discrepancies and estimation error on Airport.}
(a) First-moment discrepancy $D_1$ and (b) second-moment discrepancy $D_2$
under varying utility-call budgets, comparing OFA (IID), INSIDE-Coalition,
and INSIDE-Orbit. Lines show means across three estimator seeds; shading
shows $\pm$ one sample standard deviation (denominator $3-1$). Discrepancies
are calculated separately for each seed using equal weights across the
97 interior coalition sizes. The linear vertical axis in (a) preserves
INSIDE-Orbit's exact zeros. (c) Second-moment discrepancy versus Shapley
estimation RMSE relative to the exact Airport Shapley values. Each point is
one method, budget, and seed (three methods, five budgets, three repeats).
The dashed line is a pooled ordinary least-squares fit in log--log space;
the displayed Spearman correlation is descriptive, with no significance
claim. Panels (b) and (c) use logarithmic axes for both quantities.
